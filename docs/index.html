04 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

About

I want to know what would change my mind.

I've made market calls that sounded tidy until I tried to explain the rule behind them. I wrote about that mistake on Substack. It shapes how I research now: show who pays, who gets paid, and what would break the view.

At Alea Research, I write theses and memos, evaluate businesses, and work with founders and clients from the first call to the final report. I lead two analysts. When existing tools don't show me what I need, I build my own.

Before research, I spent twelve years in competitive football and five years in sales in Greece. Both gave me immediate feedback. A match ends. A client buys or walks away. Research can hide behind words for longer, so I try to make my assumptions visible.
